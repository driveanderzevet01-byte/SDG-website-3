Unfair work? Speak up safely.

Suara Kerja (“Workers’ Voice”) helps workers facing unpaid wages, excessive hours, unsafe workplaces and harassment. Report confidentially, get free legal advice, and we help take your case to the right authority.

Tell us what happened

Takes about 5 minutes. You can stay anonymous: we will give you a case code to track your complaint.

For sexual violence, you can also contact SAPA 129 (call 129 or WhatsApp 08111-129-129). Under Kepmenaker No. 88 of 2023, companies should handle these reports, and you keep the right to report to the police.

Payslips, contracts, schedules, photos or chat screenshots.

What happens next

  1. Within 2 working days: a trained volunteer reviews your complaint.
  2. We offer you a free consultation with a volunteer lawyer or paralegal.
  3. Together we decide the next step: talk to the employer (bipartite), go to the labor office (Disnaker), or refer you to a legal aid partner.
  4. You can follow every step with your case code.

In danger right now? Call 112 or the police at 110 first.

Know your rights

Short guides to Indonesian labor rules

Plain-language summaries. Open a guide to read more. Your contract or company rules may give you more than these minimums, never less.

Working hours and overtime

UU 13/2003 Art. 77 · PP 35/2021
  • Normal hours: 7 hours a day for a 6-day week, or 8 hours a day for a 5-day week. Either way, no more than 40 hours a week.
  • Overtime needs your agreement and is limited to 4 hours a day and 18 hours a week.
  • Overtime pay on a workday: first hour 1.5 × your hourly wage, each next hour 2 ×. Hourly wage = monthly wage ÷ 173.

Minimum wage

UU 6/2023 (Cipta Kerja) · PP 36/2021 as amended
  • Each province sets a minimum wage (UMP), and many cities and regencies set a higher one (UMK).
  • Employers may not pay below the minimum wage that applies where you work.
  • Keep your payslips: they are the strongest evidence in a wage complaint.

Religious holiday allowance (THR)

Permenaker 6/2016
  • Workers with at least 1 month of continuous service are entitled to THR.
  • After 12 months: one month’s wage. Less than 12 months: a proportional amount.
  • It must be paid no later than 7 days before the religious holiday.

Annual leave and rest

UU 13/2003 Art. 79
  • At least 12 working days of paid annual leave after 12 months of continuous work.
  • A break of at least 30 minutes after 4 hours of work.
  • At least one rest day a week (6-day week) or two (5-day week).

Safety and social security

UU 1/1970 · UU 24/2011 (BPJS)
  • Your employer must provide a safe workplace and the protective equipment the job needs.
  • Your employer must register you with BPJS Ketenagakerjaan, which covers work accidents, death, old-age savings and pension.
  • Report a work accident to your employer right away and keep medical records.

Harassment and sexual violence

UU 12/2022 (TPKS) · Kepmenaker 88/2023
  • Sexual harassment and violence at work are crimes under the TPKS Law.
  • Kepmenaker No. 88 of 2023 sets guidelines for companies to prevent and handle sexual violence, including a task force and sanctions up to dismissal of the perpetrator.
  • You can report to SAPA 129, to the police, and to us, at the same time.

How a labor dispute is resolved

UU 2/2004
Bipartite talks→Mediation at Disnaker→Industrial Relations Court
  • Bipartite: you (or your union) and the employer negotiate, for up to 30 working days.
  • Tripartite: if that fails, the labor office (Disnaker) offers mediation, conciliation or arbitration.
  • Court: if there is still no agreement, the case goes to the Industrial Relations Court (PHI).
